Executive summary

  • 1,037,391 active buyers, 87% female, skewing 45+ (median area income $94,548). The big strategic read: behavior splits by persona & value tier, not age — the age skew in quiz interest washes out in actual purchases.
  • Devoted Loyalists are the engine — ~12% of buyers but 16.0% of revenue, the highest AOV, near-full-price, and they adopt nearly every new launch first (1.4–1.8×). Seed launches here.
  • New launches are surging but hidden in annual totals — the Foundation Stick, Blushing Stick & Lip Recharge are all on ~150–175k/yr run-rates yet rank low for the trailing year.
  • Seasonality: November BFCM is the peak with an October ramp and a May Mother's Day lift; Miracle Balm is a winter product, complexion pencils peak late summer.
  • Cross-sell & pricing: the Face Pencil pulls a Neutralizer + Sharpener "pencil system" (2.8–3.9× lift); Loyalists & Executives pay full price while Recent Converts lean on discounts.

Clear opportunities to lean into

  • Devoted Loyalists are your color-expansion engine — 16.0% of revenue from 12% of buyers (top-tier $82.63 AOV). They over-index hard on The Eyeshadow Stick (1.49×) and The Mascara (1.23×) while under-indexing on the entry balms (Miracle Balm 0.51×, Mini 0.62×). They've graduated from complexion into eye & color — build a 'beyond the balm' color cross-sell journey for repeat buyers.
  • Kits win new customers and older buyers — Recent Converts over-index on The Hero Kit (1.5×) and The Skin Brush (1.49×); Comfortable Retirees over-index on The Hero Kit (~1.3×). Lead acquisition and gifting with curated kits, and merchandise them to the 55+ segments.
  • Minis skew young, full-size & kits skew older — Mini Miracle Balm's unit share falls 10.3%→8.1% youngest-to-oldest while What The Foundation and the kits rise with age. Push minis to younger/new audiences as the entry, full-size & kits to mature/affluent buyers.
  • Executives & Tech Pros are efficiency buyers at the highest AOV — High-Powered Executives ($91.69 AOV) over-index on The Mascara and under-index on Miracle Balm (0.83×). Sell time-saving 'five-minute face' full-size bundles to affluent professionals, not the balm-led entry.
  • What The Foundation is the universal anchor (14.5% of annual revenue, #1 for every persona) — the leverage is what you attach to it: entry balms & minis for newcomers, eye & color for loyalists, kits for gifters.

Method & honest limitations

Per-person (from your data): gender & age (first-name inference), location, lifetime value, and now actual purchases.
Area-level (by ZIP): income, education, urbanicity, occupation come from US Census ACS for the customer's ZIP — strong neighborhood proxies, not the individual. Persona occupation names reflect the workforce mix of where they live (shown as over-index vs base), not a confirmed job.
Purchases = real orders, but a 5-week window: Jun 29, 2025 – Jun 29, 2026 (12 mo) (1,475,304 orders, $117,458,115 gross, 98.1% matched to a known customer by email). This is a recent snapshot, not the trailing 12 months — a full-year orders export would make it annual. Revenue is gross (not net of refunds).
Coverage: gender ~91% of named, age ~87%, Census demographics ~64% (US buyers w/ matched ZIP); clustering on those 662,979. Non-US (~20%) sits in gender/age/geo only.
Age model: name→birth-year, weighted by birth volume + survival so deceased cohorts drop out.
100% free data: name-gender/age dictionaries + US Census ACS 2022 5-yr. Per-customer persona labels in enriched_personas.csv for Klaviyo/Meta. Generated for Sam Brady.
