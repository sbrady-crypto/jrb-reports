04Creative diagnosis

Early read on what's working in market (Sam → Daria, 5/15).

Top static patterns

  • Invitational / curious hooks — questions, benefit framing
  • "Tell them something they don't know" — listicle, blendability, ingredient callouts
  • Aesthetic, breathable layouts — white background, soft neutrals
  • Product has room to breathe; minimal model focus
  • NO "NEW" badge dominance — credibility > novelty

Bottom static patterns

  • Defensive hooks — "haters," "foundation has let you down"
  • Cluttered model-heavy compositions, bold colors, pink highlights
  • "NEW / Just Launched" stickers front-and-center
  • Generic claim language ("100% agreed") without ingredient backing

Video

All top-performing videos so far are from partnership creators. Mijo videos and internal videos have not yet broken into the top set — Mijo continues to show strong %-new and CTR, functioning as TOF rather than DR. Plan: graduate strongest partnership ads early into Partnership BAU.

Static test queue

  1. Bobbi founder ad"I made this because I was tired of foundation that…" — trust differentiator we haven't pulled yet.
  2. Swatch + skin-tone varietyShow the formula gliding on different skin tones. Demonstration > claims.
  3. Use-case ad"The one I throw in my bag before school pickup." "Touch-up between meetings." Specific persona moments.
  4. Review-quote staticSurface the positive feedback we know exists.
  5. Drop "haters" framingTest desirable-situation angles on net-new statics.

06What's working

  • Product feedback "reportedly very positive" (Natalie) — strongest building block for what comes next
  • NBCU expressed interest in Foundation Stick for 2026 Beauty Awards (Kenzie, Paul Street) — launch-week earned momentum
  • Yahoo wants more organic placements due to JRB's strong conversion
  • Featured in 8 new publications during launch week (Page Six, etc.)
  • Day-1 orders +21% vs Lip Recharge launch — demand is there
  • Retail brush attach 40–65%, retail teams converting well at point of sale
  • ROAS trajectory improving every day, CPA falling ~$34 from day 1 to day 6
  • WTF and Miracle Balm sales steady — no cannibalization

07What's broken — root causes

  1. New-customer acquisitionPaid Social CVR collapsed (-32% PoP) even as sessions ballooned (+80%). Meta got the eyeballs but the funnel doesn't convert.
  2. TikTok slow out of gateHistorically the best new-customer channel for JRB, underperforming. The 52.9% brush attach signal suggests the audience is high-intent when they do convert — volume is the issue.
  3. Units-per-order ceiling1.10 vs Lip Recharge 1.70. One-shade-one-stick category. Roughly half the unit gap to JETM is category math, not execution.
  4. Day-1 email delayTechnical issue pushed the launch email later than planned, costing a known peak hour.
  5. Memorial Day timingLaunching the week before MDW — customers historically defer spend ahead of holiday discounting.
  6. 30-shade decision load at $38More considered purchase than most launches. Some users likely browsing for days rather than buying immediately.
  7. Internal video creative not landingOnly partnership videos are in the top performers. Mijo + internal video need work to break into DR.

08Recommendations · next 7 days

Acquisition

Concentrate Meta spend on what's working; restructure if ROAS < 1.0 by end of week 2

  • Kill defensive-hook and "NEW badge" creative. Push spend toward top statics and partnership video.
  • Launch the Bobbi founder ad, swatch demo, and use-case angles into testing this week.
  • Re-evaluate TikTok plan — budget allocation, creator brief, or paid amplification of the partnership videos already performing.
  • Daily ROAS / CPA monitoring through 5/24. If ROAS doesn't cross 1.0, restructure rather than scale.
Conversion

Lower the friction on the 30-shade decision; bundle the brush

  • Run a shade-finder / quiz prompt on the FS PDP — explicit CRO test candidate to ease 30-shade decision drag.
  • Test Face Blending Brush as a default add-on at cart for FS purchases. DTC attach is 18%; retail proves the ceiling is 40–65%.
  • Treat TikTok-attributed sessions as a brush-bundling treatment group given their 52.9% attach signal.
Retention & momentum

Lean into the channels that overperformed

  • Email and SMS posted +88% and +117% revenue PoP. Plan a 5/19 winback flight to anyone who clicked FS but didn't buy.
  • Memorial Day comms — bundle FS into the holiday set rather than discounting the hero.
Reporting

Tighten the read

  • Pull Motion deep-dive segmentation (persona / agency vs internal / partnership vs branded / angle / format) — Huzaifa is on it.
  • Daily pacing review through 5/24 to validate ROAS / CPA trajectory and trigger restructure decision.
