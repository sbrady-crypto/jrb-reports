What this is measuring

Incremental Reach = unique people a campaign/adset added to the account that no other campaign/adset reached. iCPR = spend ÷ incremental reach (cost per net new person). Audience Overlap % = share of a unit's reach that was also reached by something else.

The goal: maximize incremental reach at a low iCPR. Anything with 100% overlap and 0 incremental reach is fully redundant — every person it reached was already paid for somewhere else.
