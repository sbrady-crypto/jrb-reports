Strategic Analysis

What the data tells us

Headline Finding
CASC FoundationStick is the single most efficient incremental reach campaign in the account.
15.8% incremental reach, 27.3% overlap, $0.01 iCPR. 1.7x the net new reach of ViewContent at ~half the spend; 3x the iCPR efficiency.
Funnel-Position Check
CASC's edge isn't just because it's MOF.
Even when isolating the three MOF campaigns, CASC wins on every metric — reach, IR%, overlap, and iCPR. Funnel position matters but doesn't explain the gap.
Audience Overlap
Foundation Stick averages ~25 pts less overlap than the rest of the stack.
37.6% vs 62.2% across all other campaigns. Strong signal that Foundation Stick is reaching a meaningfully different audience pool.
Implication
Consider scaling CASC FoundationStick spend.
$23K driving 2.25M net new accounts — likely room to push more budget without dramatically degrading efficiency. Pair with conversion lift to confirm purchase impact.
