Content-fee load · 2026 YTD

New content-fee commitments swing with signing cadence

What: of every dollar of partnership spend, how many cents we committed to new content fees that month — counting each ad's full fee (we pay it in full whether the ad runs 5 days or 30). Why it matters: shows our true content-fee commitment as spend scales. Takeaway: it swings with how much new content we sign — ~7% in quiet months (Jan–Feb), spiking to ~20–23% when we onboard a wave (Mar, May, Jun). June at 21% was a heavy new-signing month.

Full content fees for ads launched each month (each flat fee counted in full — a signed fee is a fixed cost regardless of days live or early pause; Agentio at 10%/20% of spend) ÷ that month's total partnership Meta spend (ex-justbobbi), Jan–Jun 2026. This is a full-commitment view; the 26% content-efficiency figure prorates fees to days live, which is why it reads lower.
