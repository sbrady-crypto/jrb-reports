What this is

A self-contained HTML slide deck recapping third-party creator-partnership performance on Meta for the month. Built in the JRB house theme (cream / charcoal / gold), published as a claude.ai Artifact with a built-in Edit mode + Download button so anyone can tweak copy and export their own copy. Data-driven — never hand-type the numbers.

Golden rule: copy/wording can be hand-edited; numbers, tables and charts must be recomputed from the sources below, or the reconciliation breaks.

Metric definitions the important part

  1. Exclude justbobbi everywhere — it's Bobbi's founder content, not a partner. Variants: justbobbiwithjrb, justbobbidotcom, partnershipwithjustbobbi.
  2. ROAS / CPA = Northbeam first-time revenue ÷ Meta spend. New-customer only, so values <1.0 are expected (excludes returning & LTV).
  3. Agentio bills % of spend, no flat fee → its content fee = pct × spend (col E: 10% new cohort / 20% old). Never $0. A couple non-Agentio deals are also % (e.g. brontè 10%).
  4. Agency is per-job. Don't use "last non-empty agency" — it mis-tags established creators (e.g. Amanda West is Superbloom, not Internal). Assign by the dominant / fee-bearing jobs.
  5. Content efficiency = job-matched + prorated. Match each June-launched ad's fee to that same ad's June spend (by job code, not a creator's blended spend). Prorate the flat fee by days-live-in-the-month; Agentio unprorated (already % of spend). This is the "did new content earn its fee yet" number (June '26 = 26%). Only covers the share of spend that's new content (~38%); the rest is established ads already paid for.
  6. Content-fee LOAD (over-time chart) = FULL committed fees, NOT prorated. A signed fee is a fixed cost regardless of days live or an early pause, so it counts in full the month the ad launched, ÷ that month's total partnership spend. This is a spend-burden view and is lumpy (swings with signing cadence) — deliberately different from #5.
  7. "New launches" = creators with an ad set launched in the month (Partnership CO IA campaign). Includes established creators who launched a new job that month; excludes creators running only prior-month content (e.g. Emily DiDonato). Rank by spend.
  8. Headline story = the audience-exclusion / Meta change. In June it was the June 22 change — split pre (1–22) vs post (23–EOM); partners' first-time ROAS ~doubled, CPA ~halved. Each month, find the inflection and split around it.
Efficiency vs load, in one line: efficiency prorates the fee (fee ÷ that content's own spend); load uses the full fee (fee ÷ all spend that month). That's why the two numbers differ — it's intentional, and both notes on the deck say so.

The deck — 9 slides

  1. Title — month, "creator partnerships only · excludes founder content".
  2. Partnership over time — MoM Meta partnership spend (bars) + % of total Meta spend (label on each bar).
  3. Month topline — Spend · new-ad content fees · content efficiency · first-time rev · first-time ROAS (+ CPA, customers, creators live, cohort, top source). Each KPI tagged with its source (Meta / Northbeam / Tracker).
  4. The headline — pre/post the month's Meta change; daily first-time ROAS chart + the ROAS→ROAS and CPA→CPA swing.
  5. Performance by source — Superbloom / Internal / Aspire / Agentio: spend, first-time rev, ROAS, ROAS post-change, CPA, content eff.
  6. Full funnel by partner — top 12 by spend: Meta CPM/CTR + Northbeam ROAS/CPA in one table + program strip.
  7. New launches — creators with a new ad launched that month, ranked by spend.
  8. Content-efficiency deep dive — job-matched, prorated (26%); by-source + "reading the number".
  9. Content-fee load (YTD) — full committed fees ÷ monthly spend, month by month.

How to run it each month

1
Set the window. 2025-01-01 → last completed month-end. Never include the current (partial) month.
2
Pull the roster from "New Master" (fees, % of spend, agency, launch dates, job codes).
3
Pull Polar — partnership filter (ex-justbobbi): monthly spend + % of Meta; the month's topline; daily for the pre/post split; by-source.
4
Pull Meta per-ad for the month (3 campaigns, one at a time); parse job codes; get reach/CTR/ThruPlay + per-job spend.
5
Compute per the definitions above: job-matched prorated content efficiency, full-fee content-fee load, by-source, full-funnel, new launches.
6
Assemble the 9-slide HTML (JRB theme, Edit-mode + Download script), preview to confirm it renders, then publish as an Artifact.
Reference implementation + saved methodology: Claude's memory note reference_partnership_recap_runbook. Prior month's deck file: ~/Desktop/partnership-recap-<month>.html.

Gotchas we hit

  • justbobbi quietly inflates the topline — exclude it in every pull, not just the creator tables.
  • Emily DiDonato / Lauren Youash show big June spend but no new June ad — they belong in account-wide views, not "new launches".
  • Reach isn't additive across ads (dedup) — don't sum it for a true cost-per-1k-reached; CPM is the clean cross-partner metric. Thumbstop needs Meta video data not in Polar.
  • Chart regex: two charts can share a viewBox — scope any find/replace to a single slide or you'll eat neighbors.
  • When editing the shared HTML by hand, keep the closing </div> · navbar · <script> tail attached — it lives after the last slide.
